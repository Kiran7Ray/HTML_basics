Understanding the Viewport Meta Tag

This page uses the viewport meta tag to ensure proper rendering and touch zooming on mobile devices. The content should fit the width of your device screen without horizontal scrolling.

Instructions:

  1. View this page on a mobile device or use your browser's DevTools mobile simulator.
  2. Observe how the content fits neatly within the screen boundaries.
  3. Remove the viewport line from the HTML code, save the file, and refresh the page on your mobile device.
  4. Observe the difference: the page will likely appear tiny and require horizontal scrolling/zooming to read the content.
